## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the polished refinement, sartorial precision, and institutional prestige of high-end formal wear rental and bespoke academic regalia. Designed specifically for an elite clientele preparing for convocations, state functions, and milestone celebrations, the visual language balances tailored classicism with modern digital clarity.

The aesthetic fuses **Minimalist Luxury** with **Bespoke Editorial Craft**:
- **Generous Canvas Structure**: Extensive alabaster and crisp ivory breathing room, treating digital space like fine archival cotton paper.
- **Tailored Restraint**: Structural lines evoke subtle tailoring seams, using hairline slate dividers rather than dense visual noise.
- **Opulent Precision**: Controlled flourishes of warm metallic gold that serve as badges of honor, craftsmanship marks, and calls to action without sliding into ostentation.
- **Trust & Permanence**: Grounded in deep charcoal slate hues that communicate reliability, dignified bearing, and formal etiquette.

## Colors

The palette reproduces the tactile sensation of high-thread-count fabrics, hand-stitched gold bullion threads, and refined slate tailoring.

- **Primary (`#1E293B`)**: Refined Slate Navy. Used for core structural anchors, primary buttons, major headings, and dominant high-contrast brand moments.
- **Secondary (`#B48C28`)**: Tailored Gold. Used for bespoke badges, selected states, price highlights, certification tags, and primary CTAs requiring regal prominence. Extended warm amber variations (`#C59B27`, `#D4AF37`) support subtle metallic gradients and interactive hover treatments.
- **Tertiary (`#0F172A`)**: Midnight Charcoal. Reserved for deep body copy, high-impact titles, and rich foreground contrast.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FAF9F6`)**: Alabaster / Ivory ground. Avoids sterile pure blue-grays in favor of an organic, gallery-grade foundation. Paired with pure white (`#FFFFFF`) card surfaces and subtle sand trims (`#F4F4F0`).
- **Functional Accents**:
  - *Sash / Regalia Emerald (`#1B4332`)*: Used for reserved status badges, verified institutional credentials, and fitting confirmation tags.
  - *Tailor's Chalk Gray (`#E2E8F0`, `#CBD5E1`)*: Hairline border definitions that echo stitch lines.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs high-contrast editorial elegance with pristine digital legibility.

- **Headlines (`Playfair Display`)**: Employs editorial proportions with delicate serifs, high stroke contrast, and classical authority. Display styles set a formal, celebratory tone suited for bespoke garment collections, academic sashes, and private fitting lounges.
- **Body & Controls (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Neutral, contemporary, and crisp with open apertures and clean geometric geometry. Ensures numerical measurements, sizing specs, rental durations, and policy terms remain fatigue-free and immediately readable.
- **Casing and Tracking**: Display and headlines use natural sentence case to preserve editorial grace. Category labels, status indicators, and tailoring metric tags utilize uppercase with generous letter spacing (`0.06em` to `0.1em`) to mimic stamped labels and embroidery cards.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model adheres to an editorial grid prioritizing generous framing, asymmetric curation, and structured balance.

- **Grid Systems**:
  - **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12-column layout with `gutter-lg` (2rem) and `margin-lg` (3rem), max-width bounded at 1320px for balanced garment exhibition.
  - **Tablet (768px–1023px)**: 8-column layout with `gutter` (1.5rem) and `margin` (1.5rem).
  - **Mobile (<768px)**: 4-column layout with `gutter-sm` (1rem) and `margin-sm` (1rem).
- **Rhythm & Whitespace**: Spacing follows a baseline factor of 4px/8px. Generous sectional spacing (`space-xl` and larger) separates distinct garment categories, academic credential selections, and booking schedules, giving each offering room to be appraised like an atelier artifact.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy leverages crisp surface separation, whisper-soft warm ambient shadows, and fine hairline boundaries rather than dramatic z-axis lifts.

- **Ambient Warm Shadows**: Shadows are delicately tinted with charcoal and warm umber tones (`rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.04)` to `rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.08)`) instead of harsh cold grays. This yields an organic illumination profile evocative of focused atelier showroom lighting.
  - **Level 1 (Resting Cards, Dropdowns)**: `0 1px 3px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.04), 0 4px 12px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.03)`
  - **Level 2 (Hovered Cards, Modals, Booking Drawer)**: `0 8px 24px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.06), 0 2px 6px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.03)`
  - **Level 3 (Sticky Concierge Bar, Elevated Datepickers)**: `0 12px 32px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.08)`
- **Tailored Surface Tiers**:
  - Base: `#FAF9F6` (Alabaster floor)
  - Raised Surface: `#FFFFFF` (White cardstock)
  - Inset Elements: `#F4F4F0` (Slightly warm parchment recess for input fields and garment swatch backgrounds)
- **Edge Definition**: All elevated surfaces pair ambient shadows with a 1px crisp perimeter border (`#E2E8F0` or `#CBD5E1`) for razor-sharp edge fidelity.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a **Soft (`1`)** geometry that reinforces tailoring craftsmanship—corners remain crisp and architectonic, tempered only by subtle, intentional radius cuts.

- **Base Radius (`rounded`, 0.25rem / 4px)**: Standard buttons, text inputs, badges, checkboxes, swatch selectors, and segment controls.
- **Large Radius (`rounded-lg`, 0.5rem / 8px)**: Product showcase cards, calendar pickers, fitting summary panels, and modal containers.
- **Extra Large Radius (`rounded-xl`, 0.75rem / 12px)**: Large canvas overlays and bespoke sash customizer previews.
- **Pill Exception**: Reserved solely for status badges (e.g., "Available for Convocation", "Custom Bullion In Stock") to create quick visual distinction from structural rectilinear buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid Slate Navy (`#1E293B`) with white text, 4px border radius, 44px min height. Text is medium weight (`label-lg`) with slight tracking. Hover shifts to `#0F172A` with a subtle elevation increase.
- **Secondary / Tailored Gold**: Deep gold border (`1.5px solid #B48C28`) over a crisp white background with `#996515` text. On hover, fills with warm gold wash (`rgba(180, 140, 40, 0.08)`).
- **Tertiary / Editorial**: Text-only button with a persistent 1px baseline border in slate or gold, animating rightwards on hover.

### Garment & Sash Product Cards
- Built on pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px `#E2E8F0` border and Level 1 elevation.
- Images feature a 3:4 portrait ratio with subtle alabaster background (`#F4F4F0`).
- Card footers display item name in `headline-sm` Playfair Display, with sizing availability tags and price indicators set in Plus Jakarta Sans.

### Chips & Status Badges
- **Status Pills**: Pill-shaped with uppercase micro-copy (`label-sm`).
  - *Reserved / Institutional*: Emerald green background (`#E8F5E9`), dark forest text (`#1B4332`), and hairline border (`#C8E6C9`).
  - *Bespoke / Gold Tier*: Soft warm gold background (`#FEF9E7`), deep amber text (`#996515`), and border (`#F9E79F`).
- **Filter Chips**: 4px radius, `#FFFFFF` surface with `#CBD5E1` border. Selected state switches to `#1E293B` background with white text.

### Form Inputs & Measurement Fields
- Background tinted in subtle warm tone (`#FAF9F6`), bordered with 1px `#CBD5E1`.
- Focused state transitions to a crisp `#1E293B` border with a 2px `#B48C28` focus ring at 20% opacity.
- Numeric sizing inputs (collar, chest, sash length in cm) include right-aligned unit affixes rendered in `body-sm` muted slate.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Crisp square (checkboxes) and concentric circles (radios) with `#CBD5E1` outlines.
- Checked state fills with `#1E293B` housing a gold or white checkmark indicator.

### Bespoke Fitting & Customizer Components
- **Embroidery Preview Tile**: Inset slate canvas showcasing sash ribbon color, custom velvet borders, and metallic thread preview with crisp split-pane controls.
- **Rental Calendar Strip**: Date selection blocks featuring subtle gold underlines for reserved booking ranges and deep slate fills for chosen dates.
